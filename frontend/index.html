💨 What is Air Pollution?

Air pollution consists of hazardous chemicals, particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10), gaseous pollutants, and biological molecules that degrade the atmosphere and pose severe health hazards to human populations.

  • PM2.5: Fine inhalable particles ≤ 2.5 micrometers.
  • PM10: Inhalable particles ≤ 10 micrometers.
  • AQI: Consolidated index representing daily air cleanliness.

🌱 Why Monitoring Matters

Systematic data collection empowers municipal authorities, urban planners, public health organizations, and citizens to pinpoint pollution hotspots, track seasonal trends, and implement targeted environmental interventions.

  • Early warning system for vulnerable populations.
  • Data-driven policy formulation and traffic management.
  • Evaluating the impact of urban green cover.
